About

I am a biomedical engineering researcher working at the intersection of cardiovascular biomechanics, biofluids, medical devices, and artificial intelligence. My research is motivated by the need for clinically meaningful approaches to complex cardiovascular disease, particularly congenital heart disease and Fontan circulation.

I combine 4D Flow MRI, computational fluid dynamics, physics-informed neural networks, microfluidic experimentation, and embedded sensing systems to investigate cardiovascular flow, blood-contacting devices, and patient-relevant measurement technologies.

02

Research

Selected work across cardiovascular mechanics, device experimentation, and biomedical instrumentation.

Computational cardiovascular mechanics

High-resolution flow reconstruction from sparse cardiovascular imaging

Physics-informed neural networks constrained by the Navier–Stokes equations are used to reconstruct velocity and pressure fields from sparse 4D Flow MRI measurements. The objective is to enhance hemodynamic information for patient-specific analysis of Fontan circulation.

  • 4D Flow MRI
  • PINNs
  • Pressure inference
  • Fontan circulation

Congenital heart disease

Fontan hemodynamics and flow assessment

Hemodynamic analysis, velocity super-resolution, cross-directional velocity prediction, pressure reconstruction, and assist-device concepts for single-ventricle circulation.

Experimental biofluids

Blood oxygenators, transport, and hemolysis

Fiber-level mass transport, oxygenation efficiency, red-cell deformation, OCT-μPIV flow mapping, hemolysis, and hollow-fiber oxygenator architecture.

Instrumentation

Pressure and flow sensing platforms

Embedded hardware and software for simultaneous pressure and flow measurement in pulse duplicator systems and cardiovascular device experiments.
