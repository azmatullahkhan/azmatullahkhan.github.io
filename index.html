01

Research themes

Current work is framed around cardiovascular mechanics and translational biomedical technology.

Fontan circulation & congenital heart disease

Hemodynamic analysis, flow reconstruction, pressure estimation, and assist-device concepts for single-ventricle circulation.

Physics-informed cardiovascular modelling

PINNs constrained by the Navier–Stokes equations to reconstruct velocity and pressure fields from sparse MRI and experimental data.

Blood oxygenators & microfluidics

Fiber-level mass transport, oxygenation performance, red-cell deformation, hemolysis, and hollow-fiber architecture.

Biomedical sensing & instrumentation

Multi-channel pressure and flow acquisition, wireless sensing, embedded electronics, and experimental platforms.
