Featured project

A microfluidic technique for measuring fiber-level mass transport efficiency and hemolysis of blood oxygenators.

This work introduces a microfluidic platform for resolving transport phenomena and hemolysis mechanism around individual hollow fibers in membrane oxygenators. By combining oxygen-saturation measurements, flow characterization, and hemolysis assessment, the platform enables fiber-scale evaluation of oxygenator performance under controlled conditions.
