About

From mechanisms to meaningful clinical tools.

I am a PhD researcher working at the intersection of biofluid mechanics, cardiovascular engineering, experimental measurement, and computational modelling. My goal is to turn complex flow and device-transport phenomena into practical tools for studying congenital heart disease and improving blood-contacting technologies.
