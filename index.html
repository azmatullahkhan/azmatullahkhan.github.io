PhD researcher · cardiovascular biomechanics

Azmat Ullah Khan

Biofluids, cardiovascular mechanics, and biomedical engineering

I investigate how fluid mechanics, biomedical devices, and physics-informed artificial intelligence can improve the analysis and treatment of complex cardiovascular disease.

My research is motivated by the challenges faced by patients with congenital heart disease, particularly single-ventricle and Fontan circulation. I combine computational hemodynamics, 4D Flow MRI, physics-informed neural networks, microfluidic experimentation, and sensing systems to develop clinically meaningful cardiovascular technologies.

Research agenda

Mechanistic and data-driven tools for cardiovascular care.

RESEARCH HIGHLIGHT · 4D FLOW MRI + PINNs

Featured direction

Recovering clinically relevant flow and pressure from sparse cardiovascular imaging.

Physics-informed neural networks offer a pathway to enhance 4D Flow MRI by incorporating governing fluid equations, anatomical boundary conditions, and limited measured velocity data. The aim is to provide high-resolution hemodynamic information without relying only on dense measurements.

  • Velocity-field super-resolution
  • Pressure reconstruction
  • Patient-specific Fontan analysis
